During navigation: focus on my current position, align the map to my GPS heading, and
rotate the map as I turn (heads-up navigation).

Findings:
- Rotation/tilt only happens on a VECTOR map (StationsMap.tsx:232), gated on VITE_GOOGLE_MAP_ID.
  Raster maps can't rotate -> requires a vector Map ID (config).
- Heading currently = route bearing. Chosen behavior: GPS course (coords.heading) when moving
  (speed > 1 m/s), falling back to the route bearing when stopped/no signal.
